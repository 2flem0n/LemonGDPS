## 🍋 What's LemonGDPS?

**LemonGDPS** is a Geometry Dash Private Server made for both casual players and ambitious creators.
Our goal is to create a place where creators can share their levels, players can enjoy new content, and everyone can be part of a growing community.
We're still working on LemonGDPS, so expect more features, improvements, and updates in the future!

> Fresh levels, fresh ideas, and a fresh community. 🍋

## ⭐ Rate Standards

### Star Rating
- Good and playable gameplay
- No secret ways or hacks
- Properly verified
- Decent decoration
- Level should have enough content to be rated

### Featured
- Meets all requirements for a Rated level
- Good gameplay
- Good decoration
- Overall enjoyable experience

### Epic
- Meets all requirements for a Featured level
- Great gameplay
- Great decoration
- More polished and detailed overall

### Legendary
- Exceptional gameplay
- High-quality decoration
- Strong overall presentation
- Stands out from regular rated levels

### Mythic
- Outstanding gameplay and decoration
- Highly original concept or execution
- Extremely polished overall
- A level that stands out significantly

> Reuploaded levels can receive up to **Epic**.<br>
> Join [Discord Server](https://discord.gg/KwgR2EBEeh) for more informations.
